Design brief: 2nd-order Sallen-Key low-pass filter (unity gain)

Supply: +/-12 V. Op-amp: general purpose (model as ideal-ish op-amp, GBW 1 MHz).
Requirements
- Cut-off frequency fc = 1 kHz (+/-5 %), Butterworth response (Q = 0.707).
- Passband gain 0 dB (+/-0.5 dB).
- Attenuation at 10 kHz at least 38 dB.
Tests to show
- Bode plot 10 Hz to 100 kHz.
- Oscilloscope: input and output for a 1 V peak, 500 Hz sine.
- Multimeter: DC operating point at the output and the supply current.
Use R1 = R2 = 10k, C1 = 22n (to ground), C2 = 22n (feedback) as a starting point.
