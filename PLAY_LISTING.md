```
CORE-SERVER IS UNDER ATTACK.

Hostile packets advance along the network routes. You have deployment nodes,

CyOps TD is an offline ASCII tower-defence game with a cybersecurity theme.
```

## Content rating questionnaire

Answer the IARC questionnaire honestly; these are the answers this game
actually supports.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Violence | **None.** Abstract ASCII glyphs are removed from a line. No characters, no blood, no depiction of injury. |
| Sexuality / nudity | None |
| Language | None |
| Controlled substances | None |
| Gambling / simulated gambling | **None.** Every purchase is a named, deterministic item. No loot boxes and no randomised paid rewards. |
| Ads shown to children | **Yes, and they are child-directed, non-personalized and rated G.** A rewarded ad the player opts into on the game-over screen to continue a run, and one interstitial after a lost run (only when the player declines the revive, only if the run lasted at least three minutes, and not after a revive paid with an ad). |
| User-generated content | **None**, with one caveat: the player types a "callsign" (3–16 of A–Z, 0–9, dash, underscore). Since 1.37.0 it is posted with their score to the Google Play Games global leaderboard, where other players see it. |
| Digital purchases | **Yes** |
| Advertising | **Yes** — a voluntary rewarded ad, and an interstitial after a lost run. No banners, app-open or native ads |
| Shares location | No |
| Personal info shared | No, by the game itself |

Expected outcome: **Everyone / PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone**.

**Target audience: all ages, including children.** Declare the child age
brackets in the Play Console target-audience questionnaire. This is a
deliberate product decision and the app is configured for it:

- the game collects **no age** and has **no age gate**;
- therefore **every** ad request is treated as child-directed, for every
  player, with no per-user branch;
- ads are non-personalized, G-rated, with no behavioural targeting or
  remarketing, and the advertising ID permission is stripped from the
  manifest.

Declaring a child audience commits you to the **Designed for Families**
requirements and to Play's Families ads policy. Both are satisfied by the
configuration above, but the Console questionnaire is where you assert it.

## Screenshots

Play requires at least 2; use 6–8. **Landscape**, minimum 1080px on the short
side. The game is landscape-locked, so portrait shots would be wrong.

1. **Mid-wave, board full.** Agents firing, projectiles in flight, several
   enemies on the lanes. The one that sells the game.
2. **Boss wave**, with the boss dossier panel open — shows the modifiers and
   the integrity bar.
3. **Deployment**, agent roster open, a node highlighted. Shows how you play.
4. **Zoomed in** on a dense section of board. Shows the pinch-to-zoom.
5. **Main menu**, showing mode selection and the ASCII aesthetic.
6. **Agent roster screen**, showing a REAL-WORLD / IN-GAME entry. This is the
   differentiator — say so in the caption.
7. **Statistics or firmware screen**, showing the meta progression.
8. *(optional)* **Game over summary** with a deep wave number.

Caption each one. Play shows them without context and a screenshot that has to
be interpreted is a screenshot nobody reads.

## Feature graphic — 1024 × 500

Concept: the wordmark **CyOps TD** in terminal green on the near-black
background (`#070B14`), left-aligned, with a fragment of the actual battlefield
ASCII — routes, a node, the CORE-SERVER rack — receding to the right. One
accent colour besides green: the amber of ◇ Crypto, used once.

Rules worth keeping:
- No screenshot-in-a-frame. Play crops this unpredictably.
- No small text. It renders at roughly thumbnail size in some placements.
- Keep the centre clear; some surfaces overlay the app icon there.
- It must read at a glance as *terminal, defence, waves*.

## Privacy policy

**Mandatory**, because the app shows ads. It needs a public URL, reachable
without a login, and it must cover at minimum:

- that the app displays ads served by Google AdMob;
- that AdMob collects the advertising ID, approximate location derived from
  IP, device information and ad-interaction data;
- that the advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android's own settings;
- how to withdraw consent — the PRIVACY OPTIONS control in Settings, where
  Google requires one;
- that purchases are processed by Google Play and no payment details reach
  the app;
- that game progress is stored on the device;
- a contact address.

`PRIVACY_AND_DATA_SAFETY.md` has the SDK inventory to write it from. A
generator is acceptable as a starting point, but the AdMob section has to be
accurate rather than generic.
